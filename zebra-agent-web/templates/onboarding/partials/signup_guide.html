Sign up for Oracle Cloud

Zebra runs on your own Oracle Cloud (OCI) account, inside the Always Free allowance. You do the sign-up yourself; this page tells you what to choose. Oracle's pages change, so the links are the source of truth.

Guide version {{ guide_version }}, checked against Oracle's documentation on {{ guide_checked }}.

Read first: the Pay-As-You-Go upgrade can take 1-2 days

We strongly advise upgrading to Pay-As-You-Go: it needs a card, and it should improve your chance of getting Arm capacity and protect against free-account reclamation. Staying within the Always Free limits still costs nothing. The upgrade can take 1-2 days to take effect (some people report longer).

Do not wait for it. Carry on with the next steps on the free account. Arm capacity retries pick up the priority once the upgrade lands, and the wizard shows the account as "upgrade pending" until you say it is active.

Needs verification: the 1-2 day delay and the capacity-priority benefit come from user reports and the project owner's experience; Oracle's own page only describes the upgrade steps.

Choose your home region before you click create

Pick uk-london-1 (UK South, London). Your home region is permanent and cannot be changed later. Always Free resources can only be created in the home region, and Oracle lists London among the few regions with the Always Free 26ai database (needed for vector search; Newport has no Always Free 26ai).

Oracle: Always Free Autonomous AI Database and its regions

1. What you get, and the limits

  • Arm compute (VM.Standard.A1.Flex): for Always Free tenancies 2 OCPUs and 12 GB of memory in total. Zebra uses all of it.
  • 200 GB of combined boot and block volume storage, in the home region.
  • Two Always Free Autonomous Databases, 20 GB storage each (cannot be scaled).
  • 10 TB per month of outbound data.
  • A card is needed for identity verification; Oracle may place a temporary hold. Anything beyond these limits is charged, which is why a budget alert comes next.

Needs verification: the card verification hold amount, and how long any trial credit lasts, change over time: read Oracle's Free Tier page when you sign up.

Oracle: Always Free resources Oracle: Free Tier

2. Create the account

  1. Open Oracle's sign-up page and create a Free Tier account. Select uk-london-1 as the home region when asked.
  2. Once in, create a dedicated administrator user instead of working as the root sign-in, and turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) for it.
  3. Note your tenancy OCID (Profile menu, Tenancy) and keep it for the form below.

Needs verification: Oracle changes the sign-up screens and identity-domain wording often, so these steps are deliberately general.

3. Upgrade to Pay-As-You-Go (strongly advised, not mandatory)

In the console open Billing & Cost Management, then Upgrade and Manage Payment. Free-only accounts still work, with the capacity and reclamation caveats below.

Oracle: upgrading an account and managing payment

4. Budget alert: a $1 tripwire

Inside Always Free you should never be charged. A budget alert at about $1 tells you at once if something starts costing money. Zebra's provisioning stack creates it for you in the next step and checks it exists; you only acknowledge it here.

Oracle: budgets

5. Idle resources can be reclaimed

  • Oracle may reclaim an Always Free compute instance that is idle for 7 days: 95th percentile CPU under 20%, network under 20% and (Arm) memory under 20%.
  • An Always Free database that is inactive for 7 days is stopped automatically, and one that stays stopped for 90 days cumulative may be permanently deleted. Connecting and running SQL resets the clock.
